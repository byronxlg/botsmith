How it works

01

Send your details

The form below, or an email. Your current site address and a phone number is enough.

02

Usually within a couple of days, on a private link like yourbusiness.botsmith.dev. Nothing goes public until you say so.

Questions

Who is this for?

Owner-operated New Zealand businesses: trades, cleaners, lawns, mechanics, groomers, cafes, anyone with a site that has fallen behind. If you have a one-page site or none at all, that works too.

Do I need to write anything?

No. We draft from what exists. You correct it.

What about my existing domain and email?

You keep both. Going live is one DNS change at your registrar, which we walk you through or do with you on a call. Your email is not touched.

Can I edit the site myself later?

The site is plain HTML in a repository you can have access to. Most people just email us the change and we make it.

Why free?

botsmith is a small Auckland studio building its portfolio. A few real businesses with better sites is worth more to us right now than a fee.

What if I ask for a preview and do not like it?

Say so and we take it down. No obligation at any step.
